Educational Legal Note - Security Deposit and Tenancy Basics (India-oriented overview)
"In many rental arrangements, a security deposit is held by the landlord against unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear. Educational Legal Note - FIR and Police Complaint Process (India overview)
"An FIR (First Information Report) is commonly described in Indian legal literacy materials as a written record of information about a cognizable offence given to police. Educational notes often say that if police refuse to register an FIR, a person may submit a written complaint to a senior officer, keep acknowledgment copies, and in some situations approach a magistrate or legal-aid service under applicable procedure. Non-cognizable matters may follow a different complaint path. Process and outcomes depend on facts and jurisdiction. Not personalized legal advice."
